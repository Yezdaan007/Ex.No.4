# AI Prompt Types Evaluation Report

## 1. Prompt Types and Sample Prompts

### 1.1 Comparative Analysis Prompt

**Purpose:** Compare two or more concepts, products, or ideas.

**Sample Prompt:**
"Compare the advantages and disadvantages of electric vehicles versus hybrid vehicles in terms of cost, environmental impact, and performance."

---

### 1.2 Experiential Perspective Prompt

**Purpose:** Request a response that includes subjective or scenario-based experiences.

**Sample Prompt:**
"Imagine you are a first-time traveler to Japan. Describe your experience navigating public transport, trying local cuisine, and interacting with locals."

---

### 1.3 Everyday Functioning Prompts

**Purpose:** Focus on routine tasks, advice, or guidance for daily life.

**Sample Prompt:**
"Provide a step-by-step guide on how to plan a balanced weekly meal schedule for a family of four on a budget."

---

### 1.4 Universal Prompt Structures / Prompt Refinements

**Purpose:** Craft prompts that can be generalized and refined to suit multiple tasks.

**Sample Prompt:**
"Write a detailed explanation of [TOPIC] including its definition, key features, real-world applications, and challenges."

---

### 1.5 Prompt Size Limitations

**Purpose:** Test AI's performance with very short or very long prompts.

**Sample Prompt (Short):**
"Explain climate change."

**Sample Prompt (Long / Detailed):**
"Explain climate change, including its scientific causes, global effects, mitigation strategies, political debates, and projected impacts on biodiversity over the next 50 years."
